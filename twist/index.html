About me

Not a "jack of all trades" — a specialist in a few things, and honest about the limits.

I am Twist, based in Indonesia, working remotely in application security, automation, and Web3 research. The pattern is always the same: take something apart, understand how it works, then build a cleaner version — whether that is a bug report, an automation workflow, or a web page.

I started from curiosity: decompile APKs, read smali, look for gaps. That grew into smart-contract auditing, on-chain token research, and finally n8n automation for small businesses and traders. Self-taught, no degree — I care about the output, not where the training came from.

Beyond the technical side, I also build visual assets: infographics, cover images, and dark-themed web pages. I believe a good product has to look good too, not just work behind the scenes.
